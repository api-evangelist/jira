Search your full context, trigger @Jira actions, and delegate work to AI agents in Copilot and Teams. Decisions get debated in Teams, documented in Confluence, and tracked in Jira. When these tools operate in silos, context gets lost in chat threads and AI agents lack critical context including existing backlogs, dependencies, and project history.
